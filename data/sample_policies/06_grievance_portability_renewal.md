# Nimbus Health Cover: Grievances, Portability and Renewal

Note: fictional sample data for a RAG demo, not a real insurance policy.

## Grievance redressal

A complaint should first be raised with customer care, which replies within 7 days. If the reply is not satisfactory, the complaint can be escalated to the Grievance Officer, who responds within 14 days. If the complaint is still unresolved 30 days after it was first raised, the policyholder can approach the Insurance Ombudsman.

## Portability

A policyholder can move to Nimbus from another insurer, or from Nimbus to another insurer, at renewal. The portability request must be made at least 45 days before the renewal date, and not earlier than 60 days before it. Waiting periods already served with the previous insurer are carried over.

## Renewal

The premium at renewal is not increased because of an individual's claims. Premium can change with the age band or when rates are revised for all policyholders.

## Increasing the sum insured

The sum insured can be increased only at renewal. Waiting periods apply afresh to the increased amount, while the earlier sum insured keeps its continuity.

## Adding family members mid-term

A newly married spouse can be added within 60 days of marriage and a newborn within 90 days of birth, on payment of a pro-rata premium. Other members can be added only at renewal.

## Changing nominee or address

The nominee and the communication address can be changed at any time by submitting a written request or through the mobile app. No fee is charged.
