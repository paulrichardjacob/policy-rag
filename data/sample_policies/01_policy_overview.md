# Nimbus Health Cover: Policy Overview

Note: Nimbus Health Cover and Nimbus General Insurance are fictional. This document is sample data written for a RAG demo and is not a real insurance policy.

## Plans and sum insured

Nimbus Health Cover is sold in three plans. The Essential plan offers a sum insured of Rs 3 lakh to Rs 5 lakh. The Plus plan offers Rs 5 lakh to Rs 15 lakh. The Premier plan offers Rs 20 lakh to Rs 50 lakh. All three plans can be bought on an individual basis or as a family floater.

## Eligibility and entry age

Adults can enter the policy between the ages of 18 and 65. Dependent children can be covered from 91 days old up to 25 years. Once a person is covered, the policy can be renewed for life with no exit age. A family floater can cover the proposer, spouse, up to three dependent children, and dependent parents.

## Policy term and long-term discount

The policy can be bought for a term of one, two or three years. A two-year policy gets a 7.5% discount on the premium and a three-year policy gets a 10% discount, when the premium is paid in a single payment.

## Free-look period

A new policyholder has a free-look period of 30 days from the date the policy document is received. If the policyholder is not satisfied with the terms, the policy can be returned within this period and the premium is refunded after deducting stamp duty and the cost of any medical tests. The free-look period does not apply to renewals.

## Grace period

If the premium is not paid by the due date, there is a grace period of 30 days for annual payments and 15 days for monthly instalments. The policy can be renewed during the grace period without losing continuity benefits such as waiting period credits. Claims for treatment taken during the break in cover are not payable.

## Cancellation by the policyholder

The policyholder can cancel the policy at any time by giving 15 days written notice. The premium for the unexpired period is refunded on a pro-rata basis, provided no claim has been made in the current policy year.
