# Nimbus Health Cover: Waiting Periods and Exclusions

Note: fictional sample data for a RAG demo, not a real insurance policy.

## Initial waiting period

No claim is payable for any illness in the first 30 days from the policy start date. Injuries caused by an accident are covered from day one. The initial waiting period does not apply on renewal.

## Pre-existing diseases

Any condition that was diagnosed or treated in the 48 months before buying the policy is a pre-existing disease. Pre-existing diseases are covered after 36 months of continuous cover. Under the Premier plan this waiting period is 24 months. All pre-existing diseases must be declared in the proposal form. A claim can be rejected and the policy cancelled if a condition was not disclosed.

## Specific illness waiting period

The following treatments are covered only after 24 months of continuous cover, unless they are needed because of an accident: cataract, hernia, joint replacement, kidney stones, gall bladder stones, piles, sinusitis, tonsillectomy and benign prostatic hypertrophy.

## Maternity and newborn

Maternity expenses are covered only under the Premier plan, after a waiting period of 24 months. The limit is Rs 50,000 for a normal delivery and Rs 75,000 for a caesarean delivery. A newborn baby is covered from the first day up to 90 days within the mother's sum insured. After 90 days the baby must be added to the policy as a member.

## Permanent exclusions

The policy never pays for: cosmetic or plastic surgery unless needed after an accident or burns, dental treatment unless caused by an accident, spectacles, contact lenses and hearing aids, self-inflicted injury, treatment for alcohol or drug abuse, injuries from war or nuclear events, injuries from hazardous or adventure sports, experimental or unproven treatments, infertility treatment, and weight-loss surgery unless the BMI is above 40.

## Non-payable items

Consumables such as gloves, masks, syringes, registration fees and file charges are not payable under the base policy. They are covered only if the Consumables rider has been purchased.
