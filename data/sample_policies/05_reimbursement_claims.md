# Nimbus Health Cover: Reimbursement Claims

Note: fictional sample data for a RAG demo, not a real insurance policy.

## When reimbursement applies

A reimbursement claim is filed when treatment is taken at a non-network hospital, or when a cashless request was not approved. The insured person pays the hospital and claims the money back.

## Claim intimation

The insurer must be informed within 48 hours of an emergency admission, and at least 72 hours before a planned admission. Intimation can be given through the helpline, the mobile app or email.

## Deadline to submit documents

Claim documents must be submitted within 30 days of discharge from hospital. Bills for pre and post hospitalisation expenses must be submitted within 15 days after the post hospitalisation period ends. A late submission may be accepted if the policyholder gives a written reason for the delay.

## Documents required

The claim needs: the signed claim form, the discharge summary, original hospital bills and payment receipts, pharmacy bills with prescriptions, investigation reports, and a cancelled cheque of the policyholder's bank account. KYC documents are needed for claims above Rs 1 lakh. For accident cases an FIR or medico-legal certificate is also required.

## Settlement timeline

The insurer settles or rejects a reimbursement claim within 15 days of receiving the last necessary document. If payment is delayed beyond this, the insurer pays interest at 2% above the bank rate for the period of delay.

## Queries and missing documents

If a document is missing, the insurer raises a query. The policyholder should respond within 15 days. The insurer sends three reminders, after which the claim is closed. A closed claim can be reopened when the documents are submitted.

## Payment

Approved amounts are paid by NEFT to the policyholder's bank account. Payment is not made in cash.
