# Nimbus Health Cover: Coverage and Benefits

Note: fictional sample data for a RAG demo, not a real insurance policy.

## Inpatient hospitalisation

The policy pays for medical expenses when the insured person is admitted to a hospital for at least 24 consecutive hours. Covered expenses include room charges, nursing, surgeon and doctor fees, operation theatre charges, medicines, and diagnostic tests done during the stay.

## Room rent and ICU limits

Under the Essential plan, room rent is covered up to 1% of the sum insured per day and ICU charges up to 2% of the sum insured per day. The Plus plan covers a single private room. The Premier plan has no cap on room category. If the insured person chooses a room that costs more than the eligible limit, all related hospital charges are reduced in the same proportion. This is called proportionate deduction.

## Pre and post hospitalisation

Medical expenses for the same illness are covered for 30 days before admission and 60 days after discharge. Under the Premier plan these limits are 60 days before admission and 90 days after discharge.

## Day care procedures

The policy covers 540 listed day care procedures that need less than 24 hours in hospital because of advances in treatment, such as cataract surgery, dialysis and chemotherapy.

## Ambulance cover

Road ambulance charges are covered up to Rs 2,000 per hospitalisation under the Essential and Plus plans, and up to Rs 5,000 under the Premier plan. Air ambulance is covered only under the Premier plan, up to Rs 2.5 lakh per policy year.

## AYUSH treatment

Inpatient treatment under Ayurveda, Yoga and Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha and Homeopathy is covered up to the sum insured, when taken in a government-recognised AYUSH hospital.

## Domiciliary hospitalisation

Treatment taken at home is covered up to 10% of the sum insured when the patient cannot be moved to a hospital or no hospital bed is available, and the treatment continues for more than three days.

## Organ donor expenses

The cost of harvesting the organ from the donor is covered when the insured person is the recipient. The donor's pre and post hospitalisation expenses are not covered.

## No-claim bonus

For every claim-free policy year, the sum insured increases by 10%, up to a maximum of 50%. If a claim is made, the accumulated bonus reduces by 10% at the next renewal. The base sum insured is never reduced.

## Restoration of sum insured

Under the Plus and Premier plans, if the sum insured is used up during the policy year, 100% of it is restored once in that year. The restored amount can be used only for an illness unrelated to the one that exhausted the cover.

## Preventive health check-up

The Plus plan pays for a health check-up of up to Rs 2,500 once every two claim-free years. The Premier plan pays up to Rs 5,000 every year, whether or not a claim was made.

## Co-payment

A co-payment of 20% applies to every claim if the insured person was 61 years or older when first joining the policy. Co-payment means the insured person bears that share of the admissible claim amount.
