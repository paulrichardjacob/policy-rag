# Nimbus Health Cover: Cashless Claims

Note: fictional sample data for a RAG demo, not a real insurance policy.

## What cashless means

In a cashless claim the insurer pays the hospital directly, so the insured person does not pay the covered part of the bill. Cashless treatment is available only at network hospitals. Nimbus has more than 9,500 network hospitals across India.

## Planned admission

For a planned hospitalisation, the pre-authorisation request must reach the insurer at least 48 hours before admission. The hospital's insurance desk fills in the pre-authorisation form and sends it with the doctor's notes and cost estimate.

## Emergency admission

In an emergency, the pre-authorisation request must be sent within 24 hours of admission. Treatment should not be delayed for the approval.

## Documents at the hospital

The insured person must show the Nimbus health card and a government photo ID such as Aadhaar or PAN at the insurance desk.

## Approval timelines

The insurer decides on a pre-authorisation request within one hour of receiving a complete request. Final authorisation at discharge is given within three hours of receiving the final bill from the hospital.

## Enhancement of approved amount

If the treatment costs more than the amount first approved, the hospital sends an enhancement request with the reason and revised estimate before discharge.

## If cashless is denied

Denial of a cashless request is not a denial of the claim. The insured person can pay the hospital and submit the claim for reimbursement, and it will be assessed under the policy terms.

## Amounts paid by the patient

Non-payable items, any co-payment and costs above the room rent limit must be settled by the patient with the hospital at discharge.
